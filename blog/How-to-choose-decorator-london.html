How to Choose the Best Painter and Decorator

Posted by EveroDecor • London Painting Tips

Finding the right professional to paint your home can make a big difference in the final result. A skilled painter and decorator will not only apply paint but also prepare the surfaces correctly and use quality materials. If you are searching online for how to choose the best painter and decorator in London, there are several things you should consider before hiring someone.

1. Check Experience and Previous Work

One of the best ways to evaluate a painter is by reviewing their previous projects. Professional decorators usually have a portfolio or social media page showing completed work such as living rooms, kitchens, and hallways. Looking at real examples helps you understand the quality of their finishing, attention to detail, and style of work.

2. Read Customer Reviews

Customer reviews can reveal a lot about a painter’s professionalism and reliability. Many decorators in London receive feedback through platforms such as MyBuilder, TaskRabbit, or Google reviews. Positive reviews usually indicate that the decorator works cleanly, communicates well, and delivers the job as promised.

3. Ask About Wall Preparation

Proper preparation is one of the most important parts of professional decorating. Before painting begins, walls should be inspected and repaired to create a smooth surface. Decorators often use materials such as filler to repair small holes or cracks and caulk to seal gaps around skirting boards, doors, and corners. This preparation ensures the paint finish looks smooth and lasts much longer.

4. Where Decorators Buy Their Materials

Many professionals purchase supplies from a decorator centre near me in London that specialises in trade painting products. These centres offer higher quality paints, fillers, caulk, rollers, sanding materials, and protective equipment. Using professional materials often leads to better coverage, stronger durability, and a cleaner final finish.

5. How Much Should I Pay for a Decorator?

A very common question homeowners ask is: how much should I pay for a decorator in London? Prices can vary depending on experience, job complexity, and the condition of the walls.

Typical decorating prices in London may include:

  • £150 – £250 per day for a professional decorator
  • £250 – £600 to paint a standard room
  • £1000+ for multiple rooms or larger projects

The final cost often depends on preparation work, number of coats required, woodwork painting, and materials used. Walls that require extensive repairs with filler and caulk may take longer to prepare but will result in a better finish.

Choosing the right painter and decorator means finding someone who focuses on quality preparation, professional materials, and careful finishing. Taking the time to evaluate experience, reviews, and pricing will help ensure your home receives a durable and professional paint job.
