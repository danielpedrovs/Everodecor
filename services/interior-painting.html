THE IMPORTANCE OF PREPARATION

Good interior painting is mostly preparation

Fresh paint can transform a room, but it also shows up every flaw in the surface underneath it. Dents, cracks, rough patches and uneven repairs often become more noticeable once a new colour goes on.

Marks, scuffs and loose or flaking paint can also affect how well the new coat sticks and how long it lasts.

At EveroDecor, preparation is treated as part of the painting process rather than something separate from it.

PAINT & FINISH

Choosing the right paint for each surface

Different surfaces suit different finishes. Walls are commonly painted in a matt or similar low-sheen finish, which can help soften minor surface variations.

Kitchens, bathrooms, hallways and children's rooms may benefit from a more washable or durable coating, while woodwork is usually painted in a harder-wearing finish such as satin or eggshell.

The best choice depends on the room, the existing surface and the result you want. We can discuss the options before the work begins.

COMMON INTERIOR PROBLEMS

Does your room need more than just paint?

Cracks and holes

Small cracks and holes usually need filling and sanding before painting. The cause of any recurring cracking should be considered rather than simply painted over.

Scuffs and marks

Marks on walls and woodwork may need cleaning or preparation first so the new paint covers and bonds properly.

Peeling or flaking paint

Loose or failing paint generally needs to be removed and the surface prepared before a new coating is applied.

Stains and damp marks

Stains may need a suitable stain-blocking approach, and damp marks should be investigated, since painting over an ongoing problem rarely lasts.

FAQ

Interior painting questions

Do you paint ceilings, walls and woodwork together?

Yes. Interior painting often covers ceilings, walls, skirting boards, doors and frames as one project, which can make the preparation and finishing more consistent.

Do walls need preparing before painting?

Almost always to some extent. Filling holes, sanding repairs and cleaning marks usually make a visible difference to the final finish. The amount of work depends on the condition of the walls.

What finish is best for different rooms?

Matt or low-sheen finishes are common on walls and ceilings, while kitchens, bathrooms and busy areas may suit a more washable coating. Woodwork usually gets a harder-wearing finish. We can talk through the options for each room.

Can you paint a rental property between tenants?

Yes. We work on rental properties as well as private homes, including repainting and repairing surfaces between tenancies.
