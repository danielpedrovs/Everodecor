THE IMPORTANCE OF PREPARATION

A good ceiling finish starts before the paint goes on

Ceiling painting can look like a straightforward job, but the condition of the surface can make a significant difference to the final appearance.

Small cracks, holes, previous repairs, uneven surfaces and poorly prepared existing paint can become much more visible once a new coat of paint is applied.

At EveroDecor, preparation is treated as part of the painting process rather than something separate from it.

PAINT & FINISH

Choosing the right paint for a ceiling

Ceiling paint is often selected in a matt finish because a lower-sheen surface can help reduce reflections and make minor surface variations less noticeable.

However, the best product depends on the room, the existing surface and the desired result.

In areas where cleaning or additional durability is important, a more suitable washable or durable coating may be considered.

We can discuss the available options before the work begins so that the paint and finish are appropriate for the project.

COMMON CEILING PROBLEMS

Does your ceiling need more than just paint?

Hairline cracks

Small cracks may require filling and preparation before repainting. The cause and extent of the cracking should be considered before simply covering it with paint.

Uneven repairs

Previous repairs can remain visible after painting if the surface has not been properly smoothed and prepared.

Peeling paint

Loose or failing paint may need to be removed and the underlying surface prepared before a new coating is applied.

Staining

Certain stains may require additional preparation or a suitable stain-blocking approach rather than simply applying another decorative coat.

FAQ

Ceiling painting questions

What paint is normally used on ceilings?

Matt ceiling paint is commonly used because its lower sheen can provide a softer appearance and reduce reflections. The appropriate product depends on the room and surface.

Does a ceiling need sanding before painting?

Not every ceiling requires extensive sanding. However, repaired areas, rough surfaces, loose paint and certain existing coatings may require sanding or other preparation.

Can you fill cracks before painting?

Suitable small cracks and imperfections can be filled and prepared before painting. Larger or recurring cracks may require further investigation.

Can you paint the ceiling and walls at the same time?

Yes. Ceiling painting can form part of a wider interior decorating project involving walls, woodwork and other surfaces.
