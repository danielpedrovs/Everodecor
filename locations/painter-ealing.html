Decorating w5's period homes with care

ealing's mix of pastel-fronted Victorian terraces, converted mansion flats and mews houses means every job comes with its own quirks — original cornicing, sash windows, high ceilings, and plasterwork that's often decades old. We've worked across the streets around Portobello Road, Ladbroke Grove and Westbourne Grove, and we know how to prep these surfaces properly rather than just painting over problems.

Many ealing properties sit within conservation areas, which can mean extra care around original features and, in some cases, restrictions on external changes. We handle interior painting, crack filling, and wall panelling with that context in mind, so the finish respects the character of the building.

Whether it's a single room refresh in a Portobello flat or a full redecoration ahead of a sale or let, we manage the project end to end — including prep work that's often skipped by other painters but makes the biggest difference to the final finish.
