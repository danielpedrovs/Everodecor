WHY COLOUR NEEDS A SECOND OPINION

The right colour in the tin is not always the right colour in the room

The same paint can look noticeably different from one room to the next. Natural light, the direction a window faces, flooring, furniture and even the colour of the room next door all change how a colour is perceived.

Many people only discover this after the walls are painted. A colour Consultancy is a chance to talk through those factors before any paint is bought, so the decisions you make are grounded in how your own rooms actually behave.

Because EveroDecor is a working painting and decorating business, the advice is practical as well as visual. We think about how a colour will be applied, how the surface is prepared and how the finish will live day to day.

WHAT AFFECTS COLOUR

Things that change how a colour looks

North-facing rooms tend to receive cooler, flatter light, which can make some colours appear greyer or heavier. South-facing rooms are usually warmer and brighter, and can bring out different undertones in the same paint.

Artificial lighting matters too. Warm bulbs can add yellow to a colour in the evening, while cooler bulbs can sharpen it.

Large surfaces nearby, such as flooring, sofas, curtains and adjoining walls, also reflect colour back into the room. These are the details we look at during a Consultancy.

FAQ

Colour Consultancy questions

Do I need to choose a paint brand first?

No. The Consultancy focuses on colour, light and finish. We can recommend colours and then discuss which paint ranges suit the project and your budget.

Is an online Consultancy as useful as a home visit?

Online Consultancys work well for single rooms and for narrowing down ideas. For whole-home schemes, or where light is difficult, a home visit lets us test colours on the walls and is usually the better choice.

Do I have to book EveroDecor to paint afterwards?

No. You are welcome to use the scheme however you like. If you would like us to carry out the preparation and painting, we can provide a quote.

How many rooms can be covered in one Consultancy?

This depends on the size of the property and the level of detail you need. Tell us about the project when you get in touch and we will recommend the right format.

What should I prepare beforehand?

Photographs of each room, a note of which way the windows face, and any inspiration images are very helpful. If you already have samples or colours you like, bring or send those too.
