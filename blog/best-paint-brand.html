The Best Paint Brands in the UK: A Decorator's Guide

Posted by Daniel Pedro V Sousa · EveroDecor • London Painting Tips

Choosing the right paint brand isn't straightforward — it depends on your budget, the finish you want, and the purpose of the space. After four years of hands-on decorating experience across London, here's my honest breakdown of the top brands on the market.

Premium Brands

1. Farrow & Ball

Farrow & Ball is widely regarded as one of the finest paint brands available. Its carefully balanced formula delivers a distinctive, premium finish that can elevate even the simplest room. The paint applies in thin layers while still achieving excellent depth of colour.

What sets F&B apart goes beyond the product itself. Their in-home consultant service is outstanding — a specialist will visit, assess your space, and guide you toward colours that suit both your personality and the purpose of the room. Their website offers a rich library of inspiration and practical advice for both tradespeople and homeowners alike.

Pros:

  • Distinctive, premium finish
  • Rich colour library and design inspiration
  • Personalised in-home consultant service

Cons:

  • High unit cost
  • Requires thorough surface preparation (sanding, filling, plastering)

2. Little Greene

Little Greene takes pride in every detail, from how the tins are presented in their premium showrooms to the pleasant scent added to each colour. Their palette is carefully curated, with tones chosen to complement a wide range of interiors.

Pros:

  • Premium, luxurious finish
  • Good coverage
  • Strong attention to detail and brand experience

Cons:

  • High overall project cost
  • Requires extensive surface preparation

3. Paint & Paper Library

If coverage is your priority, Paint & Paper Library is hard to beat. Despite having one of the highest unit costs on the market, its formula is rich in pigment and solids — achieving a coverage rate of over 13 m²/L, which places it among the most efficient paints available. The colours are bold and characterful, making a real statement in any space.

Pros:

  • Exceptional coverage (13+ m²/L)
  • Rich, intense colour
  • High-quality, pigment-rich formula

Cons:

  • One of the most expensive options per litre

Best Value for Tradespeople

4. Dulux Trade

For professionals balancing quality with cost-efficiency, Dulux Trade is a go-to choice. With a coverage rate of around 12 m²/L and wide availability — from small DIY shops to large trade counters across the UK — it's one of the most practical options on the market.

One standout feature is its colour-matching service. Stores like Leyland Paint in London can mix bespoke colours with impressive accuracy, giving you flexibility without premium brand pricing. Dulux Trade also offers a range of professional training courses and strong after-sales support, including a vetted list of trusted decorators verified through a rigorous checklist — a useful resource for homeowners looking to hire a reliable tradesperson.

Pros:

  • Excellent cost-in-use
  • Strong coverage (~12 m²/L)
  • Widely available across the UK
  • Bespoke colour mixing available in-store
  • Professional training and after-sales support

Cons:

  • Less distinctive finish compared to premium brands
  • Colours lack the exclusivity of F&B or Little Greene

How Much Do These Paints Cost?

Paint brand choice directly impacts your overall project cost. Here's a rough guide to what you can expect when hiring a professional decorator in London, depending on the brand selected:

  • Budget/trade paints (e.g. Dulux Trade): £250 – £500 to paint a standard room
  • Premium paints (e.g. F&B, Little Greene): £500 – £900+ for the same room
  • Decorator day rate: £150 – £250 per day, regardless of brand
  • Larger or multi-room projects: £1,000+ depending on prep and materials

Keep in mind that preparation work — filling cracks, sanding, caulking skirting boards and cornices — adds time to any job, but it's what separates a lasting finish from one that chips and cracks within months. Whatever brand you choose, good prep is non-negotiable.

Final Thoughts

There's no single "best" paint brand — it comes down to what matters most for your project. If value and practicality are your priority, Dulux Trade delivers reliability without compromise. If you want a truly distinctive finish and the full premium experience, Farrow & Ball or Little Greene are worth the investment. And if coverage is king, Paint & Paper Library stands out from the crowd.

Whatever you choose, remember: the quality of preparation will always matter more than the tin on the shelf. A skilled decorator using Dulux Trade with excellent prep will consistently outperform a rushed job with the finest premium paint.
