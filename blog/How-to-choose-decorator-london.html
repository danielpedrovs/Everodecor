How to Choose the Best Painter and Decorator in London

Posted by EveroDecor • London Painting Tips

Finding the right painter and decorator in London can make a significant difference in the final result of your home. A professional decorator does much more than simply apply paint — they prepare surfaces, use the right materials, and deliver a finish that lasts for years.

If you are searching for how to choose the best painter and decorator in London, this guide will walk you through everything you need to know, including costs, preparation, materials, and how to avoid common mistakes.

What Does a Painter and Decorator Actually Do?

A professional painter and decorator is responsible for transforming interior and exterior spaces through careful preparation and precise application. This includes sanding surfaces, repairing imperfections, applying primer, and finishing with high-quality paint.

In London properties, especially older homes, preparation is essential. Walls may have cracks, uneven textures, or previous paint layers that need proper treatment before any new paint is applied.

Check Experience and Previous Work

One of the most reliable ways to evaluate a decorator is by reviewing their previous work. Professional painters usually maintain a portfolio or social media presence where they showcase completed projects.

Look for clean edges, smooth finishes, and consistency across different rooms. This gives you a clear idea of their attention to detail and overall quality.

Read Customer Reviews

Customer feedback provides valuable insight into reliability and professionalism. Reviews often highlight punctuality, cleanliness, communication, and whether the job was completed as agreed.

Ask About Wall Preparation

Preparation is the most important step in achieving a professional finish. Skilled decorators use filler to repair holes and cracks, and caulk to seal gaps around skirting boards and corners.

Skipping this step often leads to visible imperfections, even with expensive paint.

Tools and Materials Professionals Use

Professional decorators typically use trade-quality paints and tools. These include high-quality rollers, brushes, sanding equipment, and durable paints designed for better coverage and longevity.

Buying materials from specialised decorator centres ensures consistency and a better final result.

How Much Should You Pay for a Decorator in London?

Costs vary depending on the size of the project, the condition of the walls, and the level of preparation required.

  • £150 – £250 per day for a professional decorator
  • £250 – £600 for a standard room
  • £1000+ for larger or multi-room projects

Lower prices may indicate limited preparation or lower-quality materials, which can affect durability.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many homeowners make the mistake of choosing the cheapest quote without understanding what is included. Other common issues include unclear timelines, lack of preparation, and poor communication.

How Long Does a Painting Job Take?

The duration depends on the size and condition of the space:

  • Small room: 1–2 days
  • Medium room: 2–3 days
  • Full property: 1–2 weeks

Types of Paint Finishes

Choosing the right finish is essential:

  • Matt – smooth and modern
  • Silk – slightly reflective and washable
  • Eggshell – durable and soft sheen
  • Gloss – strong shine for woodwork

Step-by-Step Professional Process

  1. Inspection of surfaces
  2. Protection of furniture and floors
  3. Preparation (filling, sanding, caulking)
  4. Priming if required
  5. Application of paint
  6. Final inspection and touch-ups

Why Hiring a Local London Decorator Matters

Local decorators understand London properties, including flats, Victorian homes, and modern apartments. They can also respond faster and provide ongoing support if needed.
