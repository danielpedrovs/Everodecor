PANELLING

How to choose a style you won’t regret
How to improve your ambience based on your personality and resilience over time

Panelling has been used for centuries to bring character and structure to a space. It has a unique ability to make a room feel warmer, more refined, and more durable. It is a powerful way to create a cosy, functional, and visually appealing environment.

There are many types of panelling, such as raw wood, paintable wood, lime-washed finishes, and different design patterns. It is one of the most effective ways to transform a space. However, if chosen poorly, it can feel heavy, repetitive, and overwhelming over time.

The challenge is not choosing what looks good at first glance, but what will still feel right after living with it every day.

What can make panelling feel overwhelming?

Some styles look beautiful in pictures but feel very different in real life.

  • Very high panels can make a room feel smaller
  • Strong contrasts can create visual fatigue
  • Overly decorative designs can feel overwhelming

The height of the panels strongly influences how we perceive a room. Higher panels can make a space feel more enclosed, while mid or lower panels keep it balanced and open.

Timeless Panelling Choices

If you want results that last, the golden rule is simplicity and balance. Mid or lower height panels help maintain openness, and lighter colours create a calm, re-energising atmosphere.

Simple designs

Bold designs work well in commercial spaces where strong impact is needed. However, for homes, simpler designs are usually better. They help create a relaxing and comfortable environment.

The safest option is to choose designs that blend with the wall instead of dominating the space.

Neutral colours

Colour has a strong influence on our mood. That is why many brands use colour to create specific feelings.

Colours inspired by nature often create calmer and more refreshing environments. The best options for homes include:

  • Off-white
  • Warm beige
  • Soft grey
  • Light natural wood

Match the space to your personality

Panelling is not only about design — it reflects how you live.

  • If you like calm and clarity → go minimal, light, and simple
  • If you enjoy strong character → add texture, but keep balance
  • If your routine is stressful → avoid heavy or dark designs
