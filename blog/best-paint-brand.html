Best Paint Brands in the UK: A Decorator’s Guide (2026)

Choosing the best paint brand in the UK depends on finish, durability, and budget. In this guide, a London decorator explains the differences between Farrow & Ball, Dulux Trade, Little Greene, and other leading brands.

In this guide

  1. Premium paint brands
  2. Best trade paints
  3. Paint costs in London
  4. Which paint brand is best?

As a professional painter and decorator in London, I’ve worked with most major UK paint brands across different types of properties — from modern flats to Victorian homes. Each brand performs differently depending on surface condition, lighting, and preparation.

This guide breaks down the most popular paint brands in the UK and explains where each one performs best, helping you choose the right option for your project.

Premium Paint Brands in the UK

Farrow & Ball

Farrow & Ball is known for its depth of colour and refined finish. It’s widely used in high-end London interiors where detail and atmosphere matter. However, it requires proper preparation — sanding, filling and priming — to achieve the best result.

Little Greene

Little Greene offers a premium feel with strong coverage and carefully curated colours. It’s a popular choice for clients who want a balance between luxury and performance, particularly in living rooms and bedrooms.

Paint & Paper Library

This brand stands out for its exceptional coverage and pigment quality. It is one of the most efficient paints available, often requiring fewer coats, but comes at a higher cost per litre.

Best Trade Paint Brands in the UK

Dulux Trade

Dulux Trade is widely used by professional decorators in London due to its reliability and cost-efficiency. It provides strong coverage, consistent results, and is available in most trade centres across the UK.

For larger jobs such as full flats or rental properties, Dulux Trade is often the most practical choice. It allows decorators to maintain quality while keeping costs under control.

How Much Do Paint Brands Cost in London?

The paint brand you choose directly affects the overall cost of your decorating project. Premium paints increase material costs, while trade paints help keep projects more affordable.

Paint Type Typical Room Cost
Dulux Trade £100 – £150
Farrow & Ball / Little Greene £200 – 300+
Paint paper & library/ Dulux Heritage £250 – 400+

Good preparation matters more than the paint brand. Even premium paints will fail if surfaces are not properly prepared.

Which Paint Brand is Best?

There is no single “best” paint brand — it depends on your priorities.

  • Best premium finish: Farrow & Ball / Dulux Heritage
  • Best balance: Little Greene
  • Best coverage: Paint & Paper Library / Dulux Heritage
  • Best value: Dulux Trade

In London properties, lighting and wall condition play a huge role. A skilled decorator using the right preparation techniques will always achieve better results than simply choosing the most expensive paint.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best paint brand in the UK?

Farrow & Ball and Little Greene offer premium finishes, while Dulux Trade provides the best value for most projects.

Is Dulux Trade better than standard Dulux?

Yes, Dulux Trade has higher pigment levels and better durability compared to retail paint.
