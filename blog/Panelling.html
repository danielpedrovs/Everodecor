Wall Panelling in London: Benefits, Styles & Modern Ideas (2026 Guide)

Wall panelling has become one of the most popular interior design upgrades in London homes. From modern flats to Victorian properties, it offers a cost-effective way to add depth, character, and a premium finish to any room.

Main Benefits of Wall Panelling

Wall panelling is more than just a decorative feature. It combines aesthetics with practical advantages, making it a popular choice among homeowners and landlords in London.

1. Adds Style and Architectural Interest

Panelling transforms flat walls into visually rich surfaces. It introduces depth, symmetry, and structure — elements often missing in modern interiors.

2. Protects Walls from Damage

In high-traffic areas like hallways and staircases, walls are prone to scuffs and marks. Panelling acts as a protective barrier, reducing maintenance over time.

3. Increases Property Appeal

Well-designed interiors can significantly improve how a property is perceived. Buyers and tenants often associate wall panelling with higher quality finishes.

4. Works with Modern Paint Finishes

Panels can be painted in matt, satin, or eggshell finishes, allowing full customisation. A professional finish enhances the overall effect.

Popular Wall Panelling Styles in London

  • Shaker style – clean, square frames, very popular in modern homes
  • Victorian panelling – classic and elegant, ideal for period properties
  • Geometric designs – bold and modern, often used as feature walls
  • Half-wall panelling – subtle and practical, often used in hallways

Choosing the right style depends on the property type, ceiling height, and overall design theme.

Best Rooms for Wall Panelling

While panelling can be installed almost anywhere, some areas benefit more than others:

  • Living rooms – creates a strong focal point
  • Bedrooms – adds a calm, hotel-style finish
  • Hallways – protects walls from damage
  • Staircases – enhances vertical space

⚠️ Cheap installations often skip proper alignment and finishing, leading to uneven lines and poor durability.

Professional Installation Tips

  • Measure and plan layout before cutting panels
  • Ensure walls are flat and properly prepared
  • Use strong adhesive and proper spacing
  • Fill gaps and sand before painting

A professional decorator ensures clean lines, symmetry, and a seamless painted finish.

Common Wall Panelling Mistakes

  • Poor spacing and uneven design
  • Skipping wall preparation
  • Using low-quality materials
  • Incorrect paint finish

Frequently Asked Questions

Is wall panelling still popular in 2026?

Yes, it remains one of the top interior design trends in London homes.

Can I install wall panelling myself?

DIY is possible, but professional installation ensures better alignment and finish.

Does panelling make rooms look smaller?

No — when designed correctly, it can actually enhance proportions and depth.
