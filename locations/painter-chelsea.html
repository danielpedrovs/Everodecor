Premium finishes for Chelsea's townhouses

Chelsea's streets around King's Road and Sloane Square are lined with Georgian and Victorian townhouses, many kept to a high interior standard. Clients here tend to expect a premium finish — sharp cutting-in, even coverage, and paintwork that holds up around original panelling, cornicing and ornate joinery. That's the standard we work to on every Chelsea job.

We're comfortable working with the premium paint brands often specified in this area — Farrow & Ball, Little Greene and Dulux Trade — and we can advise on finish and sheen level to suit period features rather than working against them.

From a single reception room refresh to redecorating an entire townhouse ahead of a let or sale, we bring the same attention to prep, sequencing and finish that Chelsea properties demand.
