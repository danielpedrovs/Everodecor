Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

How much does decorator cost in London?

It depends on the size and condition of the job — a single wall or skirting boards costs far less than a full room. Send a few details through the quote form and you'll have a price within 48 hours.

Do I need to buy the paint myself?

No — all tools and equipment are included. You're welcome to supply your own paint if you've already chosen a colour, or ask for advice on brands and finishes during the free consultation.

Which areas of London do you cover?

All of Greater London, with regular work across Kensington, Notting Hill, Chelsea, Hammersmith, Bayswater, Pimlico, Holborn, Fulham, Shepherd's Bush, Chiswick and Ealing. See the full list below.

How quickly can you start?

Enquiries get a response within 4 hours, and a quote within 48 hours. Availability depends on the size of the job and time of year — the earlier you get in touch, the more flexibility there is.
