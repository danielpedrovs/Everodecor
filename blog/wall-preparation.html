How to Prepare Walls Before Painting (Step-by-Step Guide)

A professional paint finish starts long before the first coat. Proper wall preparation is the key to smooth, long-lasting results.

In this guide

  1. Clean the surface
  2. Repair cracks and holes
  3. Sand the wall
  4. Apply primer
  5. Protect the area

1. Clean the Surface

Before painting, walls must be cleaned to remove dust, grease, and dirt. This is especially important in kitchens and high-traffic areas where grease can prevent paint from adhering properly.

2. Repair Cracks and Holes

Small dents, cracks, and nail holes should be filled using wall filler. Once dry, sanding is required to create a smooth surface.

3. Sand the Wall

Sanding smooths imperfections and improves paint adhesion. Professional decorators often use sanding blocks or electric sanders for consistent results.

4. Apply Primer if Needed

Primer is essential when painting new plaster, covering dark colours, or sealing repaired areas. It ensures even coverage and improves durability.

5. Protect Floors and Furniture

Use dust sheets, masking tape, and protective covers to prevent damage and keep the workspace clean.

Why Preparation Matters

Skipping preparation is the main reason paint fails. Properly prepared walls ensure better adhesion, smoother finishes, and longer-lasting results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sand walls before painting?

Yes, sanding improves adhesion and removes imperfections.

What happens if I skip wall preparation?

The paint may peel, crack, or look uneven.

Is primer always necessary?

No, but it is highly recommended for new plaster or repairs.
