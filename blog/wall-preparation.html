How to Prepare Walls Before Painting

Posted by EveroDecor • London Painting Tips

A professional paint finish always starts with proper wall preparation. Many people focus only on the paint colour, but the quality of the final result depends heavily on how well the surface is prepared. If walls are not cleaned, repaired, or sanded properly, the paint may peel, crack, or show imperfections. Taking the time to prepare the wall ensures a smoother finish and longer-lasting results.

1. Clean the Surface

Before applying any paint, the wall should be cleaned to remove dust, grease, and dirt. Kitchens and high-traffic areas often collect grease and marks that prevent paint from sticking properly. Using a mild cleaning solution and a damp cloth helps remove these residues and creates a better surface for the paint to adhere to.

2. Repair Cracks and Holes

Small cracks, dents, or nail holes are common in most homes. These imperfections should be filled with wall filler before painting. Once the filler dries, the area should be sanded to create a smooth and even surface. Proper repair ensures the wall looks flawless once the paint is applied.

3. Sand the Wall

Light sanding helps smooth rough areas and remove old paint imperfections. It also helps the new paint bond better to the surface. Professional painters often use sanding blocks or electric sanders to create a consistent surface before applying primer or paint.

4. Apply Primer if Necessary

Primer is especially important when painting over dark colours, repaired areas, or new plaster. It helps seal the surface and allows the final paint coat to look more even and vibrant. Skipping primer can lead to uneven coverage or visible patches after painting.

5. Protect Floors and Furniture

Before starting the painting process, it is important to protect floors, furniture, and fixtures. Professional painters typically use protective sheets, masking tape, and plastic covers to prevent paint splashes and keep the space clean.

Proper preparation may take time, but it is one of the most important steps in achieving a high-quality paint finish. Well-prepared walls allow paint to last longer and give your home a clean, professional appearance.
