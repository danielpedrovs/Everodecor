How to Choose the Right Paint Colour for Your Home

Posted by EveroDecor • London Painting Tips

Choosing the right paint colour is one of the most important steps when decorating a room. The colour you select can change how a space feels, how large it appears, and how comfortable it is to live in.

1. Consider Natural Light

Rooms with strong natural light can support darker tones and bold colours. Spaces with limited light usually benefit from lighter colours that help reflect light and make the room feel larger.

2. Test Paint Samples

Always test a small paint sample on the wall before committing to the final colour. Paint looks very different on a large surface compared to a small colour chart.

3. Think About the Room Purpose

Bedrooms often benefit from calm colours such as soft blues or neutral tones. Living rooms can handle warmer colours that make the space feel inviting.
