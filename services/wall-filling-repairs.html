WHY REPAIRS MATTER

Paint does not hide a poorly repaired wall

Holes, cracks and dents are easy to overlook on bare or older paintwork, but a fresh coat of paint often makes them stand out, especially in side light from a window.

A repair that has not been filled properly, sanded smooth and prepared can remain visible as a ridge, a dip or a patch with a different texture.

At EveroDecor, wall repairs are treated as part of the decorating process, so the finished wall looks even rather than patched.

OUR WALL REPAIR SERVICE

What is included?

Holes and fixings

Screw and nail holes, old wall plug holes and marks left by shelves, pictures or brackets can be filled and made good.

Cracks

Suitable hairline and small cracks are filled and prepared. Larger or recurring cracks are looked at before they are covered.

Dents and chips

Dents, knocks and chipped areas, including damaged corners, can be built up and smoothed where suitable.

Gaps and joints

Small gaps around skirting boards, door frames and other joints can be sealed with a suitable decorator's caulk before painting.

Sanding and smoothing

Repaired areas are sanded so they blend into the surrounding wall rather than sitting proud of it.

Priming repaired areas

Where needed, filled areas are primed so they take paint evenly and do not show through the finish.

OUR PROCESS

How we fill and repair a wall

01

Inspect the wall

We look over the surface in good light to find holes, cracks, dents, loose paint and any marks that need attention before work begins.

02

Protect the room

Floors and furniture are covered, because sanding produces fine dust that spreads easily.

03

Clear loose material

Loose paint, crumbling edges and debris are removed from around each repair so the filler has something solid to grip.

04

Fill the imperfections

A suitable filler is applied to each hole, crack or dent. Deeper areas may be filled in more than one stage.

05

Sand and check

Once dry, repairs are sanded flush with the wall and checked by eye and by hand, often at an angle to the light, before moving on.

06

Prime and paint

Repaired areas are primed where necessary, then the wall is painted so the repairs blend into an even finish.

FILLER & MATERIALS

Choosing the right filler for the repair

Different repairs call for different materials. Small nail and screw holes usually suit a lightweight filler, while deeper holes and larger damaged areas may need a stronger or deeper-gap product.

Joints and gaps around frames and skirting boards are generally better sealed with a flexible caulk than with a rigid filler, because they move slightly over time.

The right choice depends on the size of the repair, the surface and the finish required. We can talk through the options before work begins.

COMMON WALL PROBLEMS

Does your wall need more than a quick fill?

Recurring cracks

Cracks that keep returning after filling may point to movement or another underlying cause. It is worth understanding the cause before simply filling again.

Large damaged areas

Bigger areas of broken or crumbling plaster may need more than filler, and in some cases a plasterer's attention, before decorating.

Damp or staining

Damp marks, bubbling paint or persistent staining should be investigated first. Filling and painting over an ongoing moisture problem rarely lasts.

Repairs that show through

Patches that look dull, raised or textured after painting usually need better smoothing and priming rather than another coat of paint.

FAQ

Wall filling questions

Can you fill holes and cracks before painting?

Yes. Suitable holes, small cracks and dents can be filled, sanded and prepared before painting. Larger or recurring problems may need further investigation first.

Why do filled areas sometimes show through the paint?

Filler absorbs paint differently from the surrounding wall, and an unsanded repair can leave a raised edge. Smoothing the repair and priming it where needed helps the finish look even.

Do you repair walls in rental properties?

Yes. We work on rental properties as well as private homes, including making good holes and marks before repainting.

Can wall repairs be part of a full painting project?

Yes. Filling and repairs are normally done as part of interior decorating, alongside sanding, priming and painting walls, ceilings and woodwork.
