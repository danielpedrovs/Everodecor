London painting costs

How much does a painter cost?

There is no single London price because the amount of preparation, access, surface condition and specification can change the job substantially. Use the guide below as a framework rather than a fixed quotation.

Project type What can affect the price? How to get a useful quote
Single room Room size, ceiling height, number of coats, colour change and wall condition. Provide room dimensions and photographs of the walls, ceiling and woodwork.
Multiple rooms Access, furniture, preparation, sequencing and whether ceilings and woodwork are included. List the rooms and identify exactly which surfaces need painting.
Small job Travel, setup and minimum booking requirements can matter more on smaller jobs. Describe the exact item: door, feature wall, skirting, repair or touch-up.
Preparation-heavy work Cracks, damaged plaster, loose paint, bubbling, old coatings and drying time. Send close-up photographs so the likely preparation can be assessed.

Final prices should be based on the actual project scope. Materials, preparation, access and unexpected surface conditions can change the cost.

Frequently asked questions

Painting in London: FAQs

How much does a painter and decorator cost in London?

It depends on the project. Room size, surface condition, preparation, number of coats, paint specification, access and whether ceilings or woodwork are included can all affect the price. A project quotation is normally more useful than assuming a fixed hourly price.

Do you charge by the hour or by the job?

Different decorators use different pricing structures. EveroDecor can quote around the actual project scope so the client can understand what work is included. The quotation should make clear what is included, particularly where preparation is substantial.

Do you supply the paint?

Paint supply can be discussed as part of the quotation. If you already have a paint brand or colour in mind, the specification can be checked against the surface and intended use.

Can you help me choose a paint brand?

Yes. EveroDecor publishes practical paint-brand and finish guides and can discuss the suitability of a product for the room, surface and expected wear. See the paint advice section.

Do you repair walls before painting?

Where required, preparation can include filling, sanding, caulking, smoothing and suitable priming. The amount of preparation depends on the condition of the existing surface.

Do you paint doors, skirting boards and woodwork?

Yes. Interior doors, frames, skirting boards, windowsills and other suitable woodwork can be included in the scope of a decorating project.

How long does it take to paint a room?

There is no reliable single answer. Preparation, drying time, room size, number of coats, furniture and access can all change the duration. A project can be assessed more accurately from photographs and details of the room.

Which parts of London do you cover?

EveroDecor is focused on Hammersmith, Shepherd's Bush, Chiswick, Ealing, Acton and nearby West London areas. Larger London projects can be discussed depending on the job.
